## 🚀 About Me
 
I'm a Systems Engineering student from **Bogotá, Colombia**, pursuing a **double major in Systems Engineering and Mathematics** at the **Escuela Colombiana de Ingeniería Julio Garavito**.
 
I love designing and structuring **backend systems and distributed architectures** — specially microservices, event-driven design, and everything that makes systems scale cleanly. Lately I've been diving deep into the world of **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** 🧠.
 
When I'm not building APIs, you'll probably find me deploying microservices to the cloud ☁️
 
- 🔭 Currently building multiplayer & distributed systems on **Spring Boot + microservices** with agile (Scrum) practices
- 🌱 Exploring **ML from scratch**, **RAG apps**, and **RPA with Automation Anywhere**
- 🎯 Passionate about clean architecture, event-driven systems, and observability
- 🗣️ Español (native) · English (B2)
